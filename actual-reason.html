The actual reason

I love you because being with you makes even the greatest distance feel small: five thousand miles can feel like millimeters when we are calling late at night, teasing each other, sharing the weird and silly moments, or simply choosing to stay together. I love you because you listen to me and make everything I say feel like it matters, whether I am talking about games, programming, my interests, or the small details of my day. You make me feel heard, valued, and understood. I love you for your personality, for your whimsy, your caring heart, your sweetness, your loving nature, and the way you become completely immersed in the things that interest you. I love the way you let yourself be yourself around me, because it makes me feel trusted and important in your heart. My favorite memories have a little bit of you in them, and when I feel happy, sad, or close to breaking down, you make me feel safe enough to be honest without being judged. But more than anything, the actual reason I love you is simply that you are you. You do not have to become anyone else or fit anyone else's idea of who you should be. You are perfect in the moments when you are fully yourself, and I am grateful that you are in my life and that I get to be in yours.
